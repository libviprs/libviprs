Adds the three `VipsEdge` subclasses in one go, because they are one abstract op
in libvips (`convolution/edge.c:52-64`) and differ only in a 3x3 matrix. Sobel
carries the machinery, scharr and prewitt are a mask constant and a method pair
each.

`vips sobel in out` takes no options at all, and neither do the other two, so
the whole call surface is the receiver.

## What the op actually does

Dispatch is purely on the input format (`edge.c:186-198`):

* **uchar**: copy the mask, stamp `offset = 128.0` and `scale = 2.0`, run two
  INTEGER-precision convolutions (the mask and its `rot90`), then combine with
  `q = min(255, |2*(p1-128)| + |2*(p2-128)|)` (`edge.c:96-104`). That is an
  **abs sum**, not a magnitude.
* **anything else**: two FLOAT convolutions with the raw mask, then
  `sqrt(Gx^2 + Gy^2)`, then a cast to uchar.

Output is always uchar, for every input format. Bands and dimensions are
preserved and the metadata is inherited.

The two traps both bit:

1. The float arm must **truncate**, not round. `vips_cast` truncates
   (`conversion/cast.c:568`, "Floats are truncated (not rounded)") while
   libviprs `try_cast` rounds, so `Raster::cast` is exactly the wrong tool here.
   The scharr corner is the witness: the magnitude is `sqrt(2) * 130 = 183.847`,
   vips prints 183, and rounding would print 184.
2. Saturation happens twice on the uchar arm. The inner conv clips the
   recovered gradient into `-128..=127` around the 128 offset, so `2*(p-128)`
   reaches -256 but only +254, and the abs sum then clips again at 255. That
   asymmetry is the whole signature of the impulse response: the ring around the
   impulse reads 254 in some cells and 255 in others, and which cells differ per
   detector. An implementation that saturated once writes 255 everywhere and
   fails the oracle.
